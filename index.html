01 / A DIFFERENT KIND OF MARKET

India isn't
one audience.

It is many player communities, languages, platforms, motivations and spending habits — each with its own path to discovering a game.

My focus is helping studios make sense of that complexity. I’m interested in the full journey: who might love a game, where those players spend their time, which voices they trust, and what makes them consider paying for it.

I’m especially drawn to tactical, strategy, simulation, management and premium indie games — titles where the right audience matters more than simply reaching everyone.
